## 5. Merge Plan — 88 open issues, minimum wall-clock

### Strategy: why NOT "one huge PR"

Maximum batching is the *slowest* strategy here, not the fastest. Three reasons, all measured against this repo:

1. **CI is the bottleneck, not agent time.** Free runners are 2-vCPU and suites are pinned to `--test-threads=2`. PR #4154 ran **two 8m30s suites + 4m35s energy conservation** because it touched `Dockerfile`. Firing 10 heavy PRs at once just queues them. Total CI minutes is the currency.
2. **Path filters are the biggest available lever.** Verified: five physics gates carry `paths-ignore: docs/**` (Strict Energy, Surrogate MAE, `h_tr_em`, Tracked-vs-Ignored, Fast-Math), so a docs-only PR skips them *entirely*; workflow-only drops to 7 checks vs 11. → **Segregate docs and gates into their own PRs. Never mix them into a physics PR — that alone would cost 2-3× the CI per issue.**
3. **The thermal core is a serialization point, not a parallel axis.** ~14 issues touch the same ~10 files (`thermal_model_core`, `thermal_model_solvers`, `step_dispatcher`, `physics_impl/step_5r1c`, `thermal_model_iterative`). Parallelizing there trades merge throughput for conflict overhead.

Rule: **maximize agent parallelism; minimize PR count only along shared-file boundaries; exploit cheap-path PRs.**
