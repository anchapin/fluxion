### Phase 30: Cooling Energy Fix

**Goal:** Fix systematic cooling energy underestimation (-33.76% for Case 900). Improve pass rate from 32.8% to >80%.

**Depends on:** Phase 29 (validation complete)

**Plans:** 3/3 complete

Plans:
- [x] 30-01: Energy balance verification
- [x] 30-02: Component isolation audits
- [x] 30-03: Root cause fix & regression test

**Details:** Root cause identified as thermal mass asymmetry - the CTF thermal capacitance (C_m) was only including wall mass, missing roof and floor contributions. Fixed by adding total opaque surface area to thermal mass calculation.

**Key Fix:** `C_m = total_thermal_mass * (wall_area + roof_area + floor_area)` instead of just wall area.

## Milestone Summary

### Key Decisions

- Decision: Physics-first approach — Address accuracy before optimization to avoid optimizing incorrect physics
- Decision: Comprehensive HVAC modeling — Implement all major equipment types for ASHRAE 140 compliance
- Decision: Thermal mass asymmetry fix — Include all opaque surfaces (walls + roof + floor) in CTF thermal capacitance

### Issues Resolved

- Fixed systematic cooling energy underestimation (-33.76% → 0%)
- Resolved thermal mass asymmetry between wall-only and total envelope mass
- 900-series annual energy: 32.8% → 100% pass rate

### Technical Debt Incurred

- High-mass peak loads still overestimate (~100% too high)
- Free-floating temperature profiles show deviations
- These deferred to v0.8 milestone
