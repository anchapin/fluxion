# Equipment physics context
Polynomial efficiency curves (cubic degree 3) produce outputs that vary from rated efficiency:
- At PLR=1.0 (full load): Curve may return slightly different value than rated efficiency
- Example: COP 3.5 coefficient may return COP 3.0 at PLR=1.0 due to coefficients [3.5, 0.05, -0.03, 0.01]

Thermodynamic reality:
- COP 4.5 chiller should use LESS energy than COP 2.93 heat pump
- Building load: ~65 MWh thermal
- Heat pump (COP 2.93): 65 / 2.93 = 22.2 MWh (actual: 14.7 MWh due to capacity constraints)
- Chiller (COP 4.5): 65 / 4.5 = 14.4 MWh (actual: 16.4 MWh)
- Reference data (8-12 MWh) contradicts physics
