## Goal

Validate all 800-series (internal loads) ASHRAE 140 cases to ensure no regression from v0.6 accuracy. These cases test internal heat gains from people, lights, and equipment.

**Cases covered:**
- Case 800: Low-mass baseline with internal loads

- Case 830: Low-mass internal loads (lights only)
- Case 840: Low-mass internal loads (equipment only)
- Case 850: Low-mass internal loads (schedules)
- Case 800FF: Low-mass free-float with internal loads

**Target:** All cases within ±15% annual energy tolerance (maintain v0.6 accuracy)

## Notes

**Why 800-Series Should Pass:**
- Internal loads implementation unchanged in v0.7
- 5R1C thermal network validated for internal heat gains
- Automatic method selection ensures 5R1C is used
- v0.6 already achieved good accuracy for internal loads

**If Cases Fail:**
- Check internal loads definitions (power densities, schedules)
- Verify heat gain calculations (sensible fraction, latent fraction)
- Compare internal state variables vs v0.6
- Identify what changed between v0.6 and v0.7

**Internal Loads Physics:**
- People: Sensible heat (~75W/person) + Latent heat (~55W/person)
- Lights: 100% sensible heat (converts to cooling load)
- Equipment: Sensible heat (converts to cooling load)
- Schedules: Fraction of design value (0-1) by hour
