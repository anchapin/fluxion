## Goal

Validate Case 960 (multi-zone sunspace) with CTF/FD solver integration. This is the most complex ASHRAE 140 case with two thermal zones and inter-zone heat transfer.

**Case 960 Specification:**
- Two zones: Test zone (8m x 6m x 2.7m) + Sunspace (6m x 4m x 2.7m)
- High-mass construction (concrete walls, 150mm)
- South-facing sunspace with large glazing
- Inter-zone heat transfer (conduction + convection)
- HVAC: Ideal air loads in test zone only
- Location: Denver, CO

**Target:**
- Annual cooling: Within ±15% of reference (~2.17 MWh)
- Annual heating: Within ±15% of reference (if applicable)

**Note:** Case 960 previously showed 36.25 MWh cooling vs reference 1.55-2.78 MWh (v0.5). v0.6 improvements should reduce this error.

### Task 3: Analyze Multi-Zone Behavior

**Check:**

- Verify sunspace glazing model (SHGC, U-value)
- Check inter-zone coupling (conduction + convection)
- Verify sunspace ventilation (if modeled)
- Consider sunspace control strategy (shading, night cooling)

## Notes

**Case 960 Complexity:**
- Two thermal zones with different boundary conditions
- Sunspace experiences large temperature swings
- Inter-zone heat transfer depends on temperature difference
- Glazing model critical for accurate solar gain

**If Case 960 Fails:**
- Check sunspace construction (glazing ratio, U-value, SHGC)
- Verify inter-zone wall model (adiabatic vs coupled)
- Consider simplifying sunspace control (no shading, fixed ventilation)
- Document as known limitation if multi-zone coupling needs work
