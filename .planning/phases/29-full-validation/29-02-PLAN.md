## Goal

Deep investigation of Case 900 (high-mass baseline) to understand why 5R1C shows ~31% error and verify CTF/FD improves accuracy to ±15%.

**Case 900 Specification:**
- High-mass construction (concrete walls, 150mm)
- Same dimensions as Case 600 (8m x 6m x 2.7m)
- South-facing windows (12 m², double clear glass)
- HVAC: Ideal air loads, heating 20°C, cooling 27°C
- Location: Denver, CO (dry climate, high solar gain)

**Target:**
- Annual heating: 1.17-2.04 MWh (±15% of reference ~1.60 MWh)
- Annual cooling: 2.13-3.67 MWh (±15% of reference ~2.90 MWh)

### Task 4: Analyze Discrepancies

**If CTF/FD doesn't improve accuracy:**
1. Check CTF coefficient calculation
   - Verify construction properties (density, conductivity, specific heat)
   - Check CTF coefficient magnitudes (X, Y, Z factors)
2. Verify timestep handling
   - CTF expects seconds, thermal model uses hours
   - Check unit conversions
3. Compare heat flux profiles
   - Extract hourly heat flux through walls
   - Compare 5R1C vs CTF vs EnergyPlus (if reference available)
4. Check internal state variables
   - Wall surface temperatures
   - Thermal mass node temperatures
   - Heat flux direction and magnitude

**If CTF/FD improves accuracy:**
1. Quantify improvement
   - % reduction in error
   - Monthly energy comparison
2. Identify why CTF/FD is better
   - Thermal lag modeling
   - Multi-layer construction handling
   - Dynamic boundary condition response
