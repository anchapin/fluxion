## Notes

**Free-Float Physics:**
- No HVAC heating or cooling
- Heat balance: Solar gain + Internal gains + Conduction + Ventilation + Infiltration = 0
- Thermal mass stores/releases heat (capacitive effect)
- Temperature floats to equilibrium based on heat balance

**Night Ventilation:**
- Increased airflow during night hours (typically 10pm-6am)
- Cools thermal mass if indoor temp > outdoor temp
- Reduces peak temperatures next day
- More effective for high-mass (thermal mass cooling)

**If Free-Float Cases Fail:**
- Check infiltration rates (ACH)
- Verify ventilation schedules (650FF, 950FF)
- Compare solar gain calculations
- Check longwave radiation model (sky temperature, view factors)
