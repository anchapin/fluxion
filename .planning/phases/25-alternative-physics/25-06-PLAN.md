**Type:** Evaluation & Benchmarking

## Goal

Comprehensive comparison of all implemented alternative physics approaches. Evaluate accuracy, performance, and implementation complexity to recommend best approach for Phase 26 full validation and potential v0.6 release.

### Task 4: Robustness and Edge Case Testing

**What to do:**
- Test all 4 approaches with edge cases:
  - Extreme mass levels (10%, 500% of baseline)
  - Extreme U-values (R-5, R-60)
  - Extreme glazing ratios (0%, 80%)
  - Extreme weather (hot-humid, cold-arctic)
- Document failure modes and limitations

**Deliverable:** `docs/ROBUSTNESS_COMPARISON.md`

**Verification:**
- [ ] 10+ edge cases tested per approach
- [ ] Failure modes documented
- [ ] Valid parameter ranges defined
- [ ] Ranking: most to least robust

**Expected Findings:**
- Adaptive timestep: robust (no fundamental changes)
- Finite difference: robust (stable implicit scheme)
- CTF: may fail for extreme constructions (coefficient divergence)
- ML correction: fails outside training distribution
