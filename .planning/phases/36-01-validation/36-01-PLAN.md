<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Fix time constant calculation</name>
  <files>src/sim/engine.rs</files>
  <read_first>
    - src/sim/engine.rs (time constant calculation section)
  </read_first>
  <action>
    Based on Task 1 diagnostic output, identify if τ calculation is incorrect.

    ASHRAE 140 Formula for time constant:
    τ = Cm / (h_tr_ms * A_surf)

    Where:
    - Cm = effective thermal capacitance (J/K) = sum of (ρ * c * V) for each element
    - h_tr_ms = mean solar+metal heat transfer coefficient (W/m²K)
    - A_surf = total interior surface area (m²)

    If τ is too small (< 24 hours for high-mass), the building responds too quickly to loads.
    If τ is too large (> 200 hours), response is too slow.

    Adjust Cm calculation to ensure proper weighting between:
    - Air node (small capacitance)
    - Interior surface mass (medium capacitance)
    - Whole-building mass (large capacitance)
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>cargo run --release --bin run_ashrae_validation 2>&1 | grep "Case 900"</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>
    Time constant τ is now in realistic range (50-150 hours for high-mass).
    Case 900 peak heating shows improvement.
  </done>
</task>
