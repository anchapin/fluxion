# Plan 24-09: EnergyPlus Internal State Comparison

**Type:** `auto`
**Wave:** 4 — Reference Comparison
**Depends on:** 24-08 (EnergyPlus installation and test cases complete)

---

## Goal

Compare Fluxion internal states (temperatures, heat flows) directly against EnergyPlus to identify exactly where and how the RC network approach diverges from the reference CTF method.

---

## Tasks

### 1. Compare Zone Air Temperatures

**What:** Verify indoor air temperature matches between Fluxion and EnergyPlus.

**Actions:**
- Load hourly zone air temperature from both models
- Compare Case 600 (low-mass):
  - MAE should be < 0.5°C
  - Correlation should be > 0.99
  - Both should track thermostat setpoint closely
- Compare Case 900 (high-mass):
  - Expect larger differences due to thermal mass modeling
  - Identify when differences are largest (night vs day, winter vs summer)
- Plot time-series for representative weeks
- Calculate statistics: MAE, RMSE, max error, bias

**Deliverable:** Zone air temperature comparison (plots + statistics)

---

### 2. Compare Surface Temperatures

**What:** Verify interior surface temperatures match.

**Actions:**
- Load hourly surface temperatures:
  - Fluxion: T_surface from 6R2C surface node
  - EnergyPlus: `Surface Inside Face Temperature`
- Compare for representative surfaces (south wall, roof, floor)
- Expect differences due to:
  - Fluxion: single surface node per orientation
  - EnergyPlus: detailed surface heat balance with CTF
- Identify systematic biases:
  - Is Fluxion surface always warmer/cooler?
  - Does bias vary with solar radiation?
  - Does bias vary with outdoor temperature?

**Deliverable:** Surface temperature comparison

---

### 3. Compare Thermal Mass Temperatures

**What:** This is the **critical comparison** — how does RC mass node compare to EnergyPlus mass temperatures?

**Actions:**
- Extract EnergyPlus mass temperature:
  - Option 1: `Surface Average Temperature` (average through wall thickness)
  - Option 2: `Conduction Transfer Function Temperature` (if available)
  - Option 3: Calculate from heat flux and surface temperatures
- Compare to Fluxion T_mass (6R2C mass node)
- Key questions to answer:
  - Does T_mass track EnergyPlus mass temperature?
  - Is the time constant correct (thermal lag)?
  - Is the amplitude correct (temperature swing)?
- Plot time-series for:
  - Winter week (heating season)
  - Summer week (cooling season)
  - Shoulder season (free-floating)
- Calculate:
  - Time lag (hours) between Fluxion and EnergyPlus
  - Amplitude ratio (Fluxion swing / EnergyPlus swing)

**Deliverable:** Thermal mass temperature comparison (critical evidence)

---

### 4. Compare HVAC Energy Time-Series

**What:** Compare hourly HVAC heating and cooling energy.

**Actions:**
- Load hourly HVAC energy:
  - Fluxion: `hvac_heating`, `hvac_cooling` from trace
  - EnergyPlus: `Zone Ideal Loads Supply Air Total Heating Energy`
- Compare annual totals:
  - Fluxion Case 900 heating: 5.35 MWh (262-322% high)
  - EnergyPlus Case 900 heating: ~1.17-2.04 MWh (reference)
  - **This is the core discrepancy we're investigating**
- Compare hourly patterns:
  - When does Fluxion call for heating vs EnergyPlus?
  - Does Fluxion heat more at night? During day?
  - Does Fluxion have more frequent on/off cycling?
- Plot cumulative energy over time:
  - X-axis: day of year (1-365)
  - Y-axis: cumulative heating energy (MWh)
  - Shows when discrepancy accumulates (winter vs summer)

**Deliverable:** HVAC energy comparison (hourly + cumulative)

---

### 5. Compare Heat Flow Paths

**What:** Compare heat flow through building envelope.

**Actions:**
- Extract comparable heat flows:
  - Fluxion: Q_exterior→surface, Q_surface→interior, Q_surface→mass
  - EnergyPlus: `Surface Convection Heat Gain`, `Surface Net Longwave Radiation Heat Gain`
- Compare for south-facing wall (largest solar exposure):
  - When does heat flow into wall (charging mass)?
  - When does heat flow out of wall (discharging mass)?
  - Magnitude of heat flows
- Compare heat balance:
  - Fluxion: Q_in - Q_out = C × dT/dt
  - EnergyPlus: CTF-based heat balance
- Identify where heat flow patterns diverge

**Deliverable:** Heat flow path comparison

---

### 6. Compare Solar Gain Distribution

**What:** Verify solar radiation is absorbed similarly.

**Actions:**
- Extract solar gains:
  - Fluxion: Q_solar_surface, Q_solar_mass (from trace)
  - EnergyPlus: `Surface Solar Radiation Heat Gain`
- Compare total solar gain (should match closely)
- Compare distribution:
  - How much absorbed at surface vs deep in mass?
  - Fluxion: split between surface and mass nodes
  - EnergyPlus: absorbed at surface, conducted through CTF
- Compare timing:
  - Solar gain peaks at same time?
  - Thermal response (temperature rise) at same time?

**Deliverable:** Solar gain distribution comparison

---

### 7. Analyze Discrepancy Patterns

**What:** Identify systematic patterns in where Fluxion diverges from EnergyPlus.

**Actions:**
- Create discrepancy time-series:
  - ΔT_interior = T_fluxion - T_energyplus (hourly)
  - ΔQ_HVAC = Q_fluxion - Q_energyplus (hourly)
- Correlate with driving forces:
  - Outdoor temperature (does error grow when T_out is extreme?)
  - Solar radiation (does error grow with high solar?)
  - Wind speed (does error grow with high infiltration?)
- Identify patterns:
  - **Lag error:** Fluxion response delayed relative to EnergyPlus
  - **Amplitude error:** Fluxion swings more/less than EnergyPlus
  - **Bias error:** Fluxion consistently warmer/cooler
  - **Accumulation error:** Small hourly errors compound over time
- Quantify each error type

**Deliverable:** Discrepancy pattern analysis

---

  - **Recommendations:**
    - Should Fluxion continue with RC networks?
    - Should Fluxion adopt CTF or finite difference?
    - Should Fluxion use hybrid RC + ML correction?

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive comparison report
