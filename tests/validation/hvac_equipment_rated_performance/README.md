# HVAC BESTEST validation scaffold

This directory is the integration-test home for Fluxion's HVAC BESTEST work. Issue
#1754 provides module and CI wiring only: no case inputs, reference bounds,
acceptance tolerances, or equipment calculations are implemented here.

## Taxonomy

HVAC BESTEST distinguishes validation methods by the source of truth:

- **Analytical verification** compares a program with a known analytical or
  generally accepted numerical solution under tightly controlled boundary
  conditions. For HVAC BESTEST, these are not free-floating envelope tests: the
  HVAC equipment or air-distribution system is active and the solution checks
  mass and energy balances, coil loads, fan heat, and related state points.
- **Comparative testing** compares a program with itself or with qualified,
  independently implemented reference programs when no analytical truth standard
  is available. Comparative ranges are evidence, not physical constants, and must
  retain source/version provenance.

Envelope-only and free-floating cases remain in the ASHRAE Standard 140 harness;
they validate the building loads presented to HVAC models but are not RP-865 HVAC
case identifiers.

## Case families

| Family | Method | Scope | Planned module |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AE101`–`AE445` | Analytical / quasi-analytical | RP-865-derived airside HVAC equipment and distribution-system configurations | `analytical.rs` |
| `CE100`–`CE200` | Analytical | ASHRAE 140-2023 §9 unitary space-cooling equipment cases (140-2023 identifiers; IEA SHC Task 22 originally published the same family as `E100`–`E200`); dry-coil cases `CE100`–`CE140`, wet-coil cases `CE150`–`CE200`. Case definitions generated by `scripts/generate_section9_ce_cases.py` from the in-repo normative performance map and the NREL BESTEST-GSR parameter table (#4296). The §9 engine-run harness landed: every one of the 14 cases is dispatched through the CE100 performance map (`section9_engine_run.rs`) and scored against its own per-case band — the Task-22 comparative ensemble bounds, since the §9.2 quasi-analytical criteria stay `null` pending the licensed 140-2023 §9 materials (#4285). The per-case rows (band kind, band source, engine value, band edges, gap, status) live in `data/section9_engine_run_results.json`; the known-fail rows and the remaining blockers are tracked in KNOWN_ISSUES §LIMIT-42. Every one of the 14 cases states its band status explicitly in the generated case table: `acceptance.analytical` (the §9.2 quasi-analytical slot, `null` + reason), `acceptance.comparative` (the Task-22 ensemble band with its source file) and, for the five cases the bounds CSV does not cover (CE165/CE180/CE185/CE190/CE195), an explicit `acceptance.comparative_note` naming the missing source. The generator validates the transcribed CE100 performance map (24 points per table: 6 ODB rows × 4 EWB columns) and the reconciled ARI rating point before writing the table, so a map edit cannot silently change what the cases dispatch against. NOTE: the current `analytical.rs` "E100/E200/E300" entries are a synthetic equipment self-consistency taxonomy, NOT these §9 cases — see `analytical.rs` | `analytical.rs` (synthetic); §9 harness in `section9_engine_run.rs` |
| Future dynamic cases | Comparative | Cases whose dynamic boundary conditions prevent a closed-form truth solution | `comparative.rs` |

ASHRAE Standard 140 building-fabric identifiers such as Cases 600 and 900 are
intentionally excluded from this taxonomy; they remain under the existing envelope
validation suite.

## Module responsibilities

- `analytical.rs`: analytical and quasi-analytical case runners and assertions.
- `comparative.rs`: cross-program comparative cases and qualified comparison bands.
- `reference_data.rs`: typed loaders with publication, version, units, and checksum
  provenance for RP-865/HVAC BESTEST reference data.
- `section9_ce.rs`: ASHRAE 140 §9 CE-case steady-state dispatch through the CE100
  performance map.
- `section9_engine_run.rs`: the §9 engine-run harness — every CE100–CE200 case
  dispatched through that map and scored against its own sourced band (band kind and
  source recorded per row), with the known-fail rows recorded in
  `data/section9_engine_run_results.json`.
- `mod.rs`: integration-test target root only.

Follow-on issues must add tests before implementation, preserve moist-air and dry-air
mass balances, close the sensible/latent energy balance, and avoid invented bounds
or placeholder correlations.
